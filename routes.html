      Text, reconstruction, calculation

      Sam Mirelman, Tuning Procedures in Ancient Iraq (2013), figure 5 on p. 52 and pp. 53–55, presents the tuning network and discusses the limits of a modern interpretation.

      Jay Rahn, Was Mesopotamian Tuning Diatonic? (2022), paragraphs 11–16, discusses both directions and repeated cycles. Paragraph 45 explains the editorial basis of the tightening terminology.

      nê’um means “loosen.” The contextual tightening reading is nasāḫum; its chapter is extensively restored. The words and their preservation are part of the evidence, while the exact fractions are our calculation.

      The 4/3 and 3/2 frequency ratios, hertz reference, exact arithmetic, synthetic timbre and comparison transposition are modern choices. Neither the numerical gap nor the transposition is a measurement or prescription preserved on a tablet.
