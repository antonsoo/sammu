# Retained experiments

These are captured examples, not a mirror of the latest hosted build. Their
edition IDs are preserved inside the JSON and HTML. The three illustrated
readings contain the exact records saved beside them; the route artifacts also
match the byte identities in [their original receipt](../docs/routes-receipt.json).

| Files | Captured experiment | Edition prefix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `retuning-study.*`, `retuning-history.csv`, `retuning-diagram.svg` | Two tightening operations from the initial tuning | `cf03122b` |
| `practice-partials-110.*` | Two loosening operations, followed by a tentative 110-cent loosening; isolated partials | `80387d6a` |
| `routes/*` | Seven prior loosening moves, then opposite roads to embūbum; heard B transposed to A | `9d214e48` |
| `live-adjustment.json`, `live-adjustment.wav` | Actual continuous browser-output excerpt, beginning while already sounding | Source commit recorded in `runtimeSource` |

The live excerpt is separate from the normal fixed-setting WAV export. Its
record identifies the sample rate, exact sample count, observed control events,
source revision and WAV digest. It is synthetic browser output, not a recording
of an ancient instrument or evidence of ancient performance.

The older bench/practice HTML and PDFs keep their original MIT notice and
historical author attribution; the HTML is byte-identical to its original
committed capture. The [original MIT terms](MIT-LICENSE.txt) accompany those
versions. Current bench/practice exports instead name PolyForm Noncommercial
1.0.0 and embed its complete terms, alongside the complete font notices. The
route examples already carry those newer terms. To obtain a current reading, reopen the JSON's
experiment URL (the nested `practice.experiment` for the practice attempt),
then choose **Save illustrated reading** or **Save a study**. The URL restores
the experiment in the current edition; it does not restore the old application
binary. Historical arithmetic and modern sound choices remain separate from
the reconstructed procedure.

The [published audit](../docs/navigation-transfer-publication.md) records the
newer public edition and its actual downloads, native-file and transport-cut
checks. Current hosted screenshots and receipts do not relabel these snapshots
as newly generated examples.
