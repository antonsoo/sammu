This is an exploration, with no hearing score. 100 cents is one modern equal-tempered semitone.

Follow the sound.

  1. Listen to the unchanged pair, then move the control a little.
  2. Compare the two settings. Try an adjustment on either side of where the sound seems to settle.
  3. Isolate the selected partials to hear their interaction directly, then return to the fuller sound.

Changing one string changes several partial frequencies at once. A smooth patch in a synthetic signal helps you explore that relationship; it does not recover an ancient listener’s judgment.
