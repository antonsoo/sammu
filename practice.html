Follow the sound.

  1. Press Listen, then move the adjustment while the pair keeps sounding.
  2. Listen for the beats slowing down, then speeding up again as you move past the alignment. Slow down near the place they seem to settle.
  3. Isolate the selected partials without stopping. Compare with the unchanged setting when you want a fresh reference.

A match stops the isolated pair’s beats; its loudness still depends on phase. In the fuller sound, several partials interact at once. This synthetic exercise does not recover an ancient listener’s judgment.
